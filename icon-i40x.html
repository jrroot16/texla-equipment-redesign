Icon i40X

The ICON i40X Four-Seater Golf Cart is crafted for friends who believe the journey should be as fun as the destination. With spacious seating, modern styling, and advanced comfort features, it's the perfect cart for neighborhood cruising, resort exploring, or golf course outings. Designed to keep everyone connected, the ICON i40X transforms ordinary rides into memorable adventures, making every trip with friends feel stylish, comfortable, and unforgettable.

This model comes standard in a non-lifted configuration.

The cart can be lifted upon request, which requires a labor charge and swapping to tires appropriate for the new configuration.
