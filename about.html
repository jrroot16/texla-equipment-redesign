ABOUT TEXLA

Locally Owned. Proudly Serving Southeast Texas.

BUILT ON LOCAL ROOTS & INDUSTRY EXPERTISE

Texla Equipment was founded by Jared Bishop, a Beaumont native with over 25 years of experience in the industrial equipment industry. Born from a need for a locally-owned dealership that could provide prompt, personalized service, Texla has grown to become Southeast Texas's trusted source for forklift sales, rentals, service, and parts.

With two locations in Beaumont and Lufkin, we're positioned to deliver fast response times and hands-on support to businesses across the region.
